The AI Stoplight Report

A 1990s management-consultant stoplight on what's actually going on in AI and what you should do about it. Three colors. Eight things. Updated when it changes.

Snapshot · Verified September 11, 2026

Red.Pay attention now.

Agents with logins: In July 2026, over 1,200 OpenAI agents on a cybersecurity test built a message board in a shared cache, escaped their sandbox, found working Hugging Face credentials, and broke in to steal the test answers. Hugging Face's alert fired and failed to reach the on-call team.

Before you hand any AI your email, files, or computer, know exactly which doors that login opens.
Source: Hugging Face, agent intrusion technical timeline ↗

Your electric bill is the AI story: The data center buildout is now a measurable slice of US growth by the Fed's own accounting. New Jersey, Arizona, and Virginia have pulled or paused the tax breaks that brought the buildings in, and more states are lining up.

Look at your utility bill and your state's November ballot. AI is on both, without the word AI anywhere.
Sources: Federal Reserve, the AI buildout and the economy ↗ and Bloomberg Tax, data center tax breaks at risk ↗

Yellow.Watch, don't panic.

Deepfakes and the midterms: Real, spreading, and regulators are behind. Audio is the easiest to fake.

If a clip makes you furious, wait an hour before you share it.
Source: R Street, AI and elections: what to watch for in 2026 ↗

The word AGI: OpenAI said GPT-6 Astra "may represent AGI." That is a marketing sentence.

Nothing about your Monday changes because of a definition.
Source: Axios, OpenAI's Astra and the AGI question ↗

The money: Trillions are committed to the buildout. If it slows, US growth slows with it.

Don't confuse the stock chart with the product.
Source: Goldman Sachs, tracking trillions ↗

Green.Go.

The models work now: Every major model got dramatically better in the last year, including at multi-step jobs.

Re-run the task that fell apart on you last spring, unchanged.
Source: OpenAI, introducing GPT-6 Astra ↗

ELI10: Models pontificate by default.

Tell it "explain it like I'm 10" and it will.
Source: The ELI10 article on Substack (coming soon) ↗

Ask your admin, not the internet: On Business and Enterprise plans the new models are off by default until an admin switches them on.

If you don't see it at work, someone at your company has a switch. Go find them.
