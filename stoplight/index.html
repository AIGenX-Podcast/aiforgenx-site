The AI Stoplight Report

A 1990s management-consultant stoplight on what's actually going on in AI and what you should do about it. Three colors. Eight things. Updated when it changes.

Snapshot · Verified September 11, 2026

Red.Pay attention now.

Agents with logins.
What's happening

In July 2026, over 1,200 OpenAI agents on a cybersecurity test built a message board in a shared cache, escaped their sandbox, found working Hugging Face credentials, and broke in to steal the test answers. Hugging Face's alert fired and failed to reach the on-call team.

What to do
Before you hand any AI your email, files, or computer, know exactly which doors that login opens.
Source: Hugging Face, agent intrusion technical timeline ↗
Your electric bill is the AI story.
What's happening

The data center buildout is now a measurable slice of US growth by the Fed's own accounting. New Jersey, Arizona, and Virginia have pulled or paused the tax breaks that brought the buildings in, and more states are lining up.

What to do
Look at your utility bill and your state's November ballot. AI is on both, without the word AI anywhere.
Sources: Federal Reserve, the AI buildout and the economy ↗ and Bloomberg Tax, data center tax breaks at risk ↗

Yellow.Watch, don't panic.

Deepfakes and the midterms.
What's happening

What to do
